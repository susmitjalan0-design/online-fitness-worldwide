What a month of Lean Reset looks like

  • 20 sessions a month. 1:1. Coached live.
  • Nothing is pulled from a generic fat-loss sheet. Each session is planned for that week.
  • Strength work and moving more, together. WHO asks adults for 150 to 300 minutes a week of moderate activity, plus muscle-strengthening work on 2 or more days. WHO guidelines, 2020.
  • On Google Meet, Zoom or FaceTime. In Hindi or English. On IST.

Training is one part

Food, sleep and daily steps matter too. We talk about them on the call. For a medical condition or a clinical diet, see your doctor or a registered dietitian.

Questions

Is there a fixed fat-loss plan?

No. Nothing is pulled from a generic fat-loss sheet. Each session is planned for that week and coached live.

How many sessions is Lean Reset?

20 sessions a month, 1:1, coached live on Google Meet, Zoom or FaceTime.

Can I do it at home?

Yes. A phone, floor space the size of a mat, and whatever kit you have. The session is written for the room you are in.

What does it cost?

Not on this page. Booking holds a time. The fee is agreed directly, before or on the first call.

Do you promise a number on the scale?

No. We do not promise results and we do not publish before-and-after numbers. We coach the sessions and adjust them every week.

Where do I start?

A 30-minute consultation. It is a conversation, not training. It ends with a clear next step.

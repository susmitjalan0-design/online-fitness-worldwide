  1. 1The format

    HYROX
    Run 1 km, then one station. Eight times.[1]
    DEKA
    Ten zones in a fixed order. FIT has a 500 m run before each zone. MILE has 160 m. STRONG has no running.[3][4]
    ATHX
    A 2.5-hour session with six zones and three scored workouts: Strength, Endurance and MetCon X. Solo or pairs.[5]

  2. 2The running

    HYROX
    8,000 m in eight parts. If the venue makes a 1 km run several laps, the full distance is still run.[2]
    DEKA
    FIT: 5,000 m. MILE: 1,600 m. STRONG: none.[4]
    ATHX
    No separate run leg. In 2026 Endurance mixes running and rowing for distance. In 2027 you run a set distance, then SkiErg for distance.[6][7]

  3. 3How you are scored

    HYROX
    Finish time. Two-minute penalties, for example for the wrong station.[2]
    DEKA
    Finish time plus penalties. Examples: 10 seconds per missed rep in zone 1, and 1:30 per missed push-and-pull length on the sled in zone 9. Less than 80% of a zone is an unofficial finish.[4]
    ATHX
    Three scores. In 2026: total weight lifted for Strength, total distance for Endurance, and time for MetCon X.[6]

  4. 4Shared movements and loads

    HYROX
    SkiErg 1,000 m. Sled push 50 m and pull 50 m. Row 1,000 m. Farmers carry 200 m: Open men 2 × 24 kg (53 lb), Open women 2 × 16 kg (35 lb). Burpee broad jumps 80 m.[1][2]
    DEKA
    Row 500 m. Ski 500 m. Sled push and pull 100 m, on a magnetic sled. Farmers carry 100 m: men 27.5 kg (60 lb) per hand, women 17.5 kg (40 lb) per hand. Weighted burpees, 20 reps.[4]
    ATHX
    The 2026 MetCon X has SkiErg, sandbag carry, walking lunges and burpee broad jumps.[6] The 2027 one swaps in RowErg, bench press and sandbag squats.[7]

  5. 5Typical times

    HYROX
    Third party, from 1,274,586 official finishes: typical Open men 1:27:59, Open women 1:36:00.[10] I found no official HYROX average.
    DEKA
    Third party, from DEKA's 2026 leaderboard: median FIT time 48:20 for men and 54:32 for women. That analysis says DEKA publishes no average time.[11]
    ATHX
    No single finish time, so no matching benchmark. I found none published.

  6. 6Where it runs

    HYROX
    Everywhere. On 2 November 2026 the finder listed 66 events and 36 country options across five continents.[9] For example: London 2 to 6 Dec 2026, Dallas 18 to 22 Nov 2026, Dubai 27 to 28 Nov 2026, Singapore 26 to 29 Nov 2026.[9]
    DEKA
    On the official pages I opened: the US (Atlanta 7 to 8 Nov 2026, Denver 27 Mar 2027), Spain and Portugal (Tenerife 21 to 22 Nov 2026, Lisboa 15 May 2027), and the World Championship in Fort Worth, 10 to 13 Dec 2026.[12][13][14] Past: Dubai on 26 Sep 2026 and Bangkok on 1 Nov 2026.[16][17] I could not confirm a UK date on an official page.
    ATHX
    36 events in 14 countries on 2 November 2026, in Europe and the US. Examples: Amsterdam and Los Angeles 7 Nov 2026, the Finals in Lisbon 27 to 29 Nov 2026, London 23 to 24 Jan 2027.[8]

  7. 7Who can enter, and the divisions

    HYROX
    At least 16 on race day. Open, Pro, Doubles and Relay.[1][2]
    DEKA
    Adults from 14, and youth 10 to 13 with lighter loads. Elite and Age Group, and teams of 2 or 4. Your age is your age on 30 September of the season.[4]
    ATHX
    LITE, ATHX and PRO. Solo, or in pairs.[5]

How I'd choose, and what I won't claim

You like a fixed course, a clock and a global calendar: look at HYROX.[1][9]

You like ten fixed zones, with a short run or none: look at DEKA.[3]

You like heavy lifts, an engine test and a circuit in one session: look at ATHX.[5]

India: HYROX lists Gujarat on 26 to 28 November 2026 and Noida on 15 to 17 January 2027.[9][15] I found no DEKA or ATHX listing in India.

Times here are third party and not comparable across formats. No client results, none implied. No prices.

A sample taster day, to feel the difference

Sample only. Not a prescription. Adjust it to your body, your health and your history.

  • Warm-up, 10 minutes.
  • HYROX taste: 1 km easy run, 500 m SkiErg, 1 km easy run, 100 m carry.
  • DEKA taste: 20 box step-overs, 500 m row, 25 calories on a bike, 10 light burpees.
  • ATHX taste: 5 squats at a moderate load, 3 minutes of run and row, 10 burpee broad jumps.
  • Note which one you enjoyed. Stop when form breaks. It is a taste, not a test.

Before you start

General information, not medical advice. ACSM's screening model says: if you are inactive, have no symptoms and no known heart, metabolic or kidney disease, you can start light to moderate exercise without medical clearance and build up gradually. If you have one of those conditions, or symptoms such as chest pain, unusual breathlessness or dizziness, get medical clearance first.[18] Race-style work is hard: heavy lifts, jumps, burpees, sprints and carries. Build up to it over weeks. Don't test a maximum lift alone or without a spotter or a coach watching. Sleds, carries, burpees and heavy lifts are hard on knees, back and shoulders. Build up over weeks. Ask a doctor first if you have a heart, blood-pressure or joint condition.

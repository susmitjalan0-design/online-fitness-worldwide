HYROX times and results: what published result data show

What peer-reviewed analyses of official HYROX results, and a simulated-race study, report on finish times, splits and pacing.

By Susmit Jalan, strength and conditioning coach at Online Fitness Worldwide. Updated 21 November 2026

An analysis of 39,696 individual PRO and ELITE results from HYROX's first seven seasons (2018/19 to 2024/25) found that running accounted for about 50% of total race time. ELITE median times improved from 1:06:24 to 0:57:17 for men and from 1:11:09 to 1:03:22 for women.[1]

A separate study extracted 186,411 results from the official HYROX ranking database up to the 2023/24 season and published mean and 10th-to-90th percentile tables for each category and each segment.[2] In a simulated Open race, 11 recreational athletes had a median time of 86.5 minutes, 51.2 on the runs and 32.8 on the stations.[5]

HYROX publishes race results on its official results site.[6]

Data sets and methods

The seven-season analysis used publicly available results from 11,842 women and 27,854 men. In the top 100 results per season, total time improved by about 13 minutes (about 19%) for men and 17 minutes (about 21%) for women between seasons 1 and 7.[1]

The Madrid study measured the 24 athletes in a laboratory within 10 days of the race; their race time averaged 69:58, and mean running intensity was 84% of the second ventilatory threshold.[3] A participation analysis counted 278,063 participants across 145 races and 5 seasons, with 22 host countries in total.[4]

What the guidelines and sources state on safety

ACSM's 2015 screening model bases pre-exercise medical clearance on current activity, signs or symptoms and/or known cardiovascular, metabolic or renal disease, and desired intensity.[7]

Frequently asked questions

What is a typical HYROX finish time?

A study of 186,411 official results published percentile tables by category and segment.[2] In a simulated Open race, 11 recreational athletes had a median of 86.5 minutes.[5]

How fast are ELITE HYROX times?

Across seven seasons, ELITE median times improved from 1:06:24 to 0:57:17 for men and from 1:11:09 to 1:03:22 for women.[1]

How much of a HYROX race is running?

About 50% of total race time in PRO and ELITE results;[1] in the simulated Open race, 51.2 of 86.5 minutes.[5]

How does pace change across the runs?

In 24 PRO men at HYROX Madrid 2025, running speed declined from run 1 to run 8, with the lowest speed in the Roxzone.[3]

Kettlebells and HYROX: what the rulebook and research show

The kettlebell station in the official HYROX singles rulebook, its weights and standards, and what published kettlebell training studies measured.

By Susmit Jalan, strength and conditioning coach at Online Fitness Worldwide. Updated 21 November 2026

In HYROX, kettlebells are used at one station: the 200 m farmers carry. The official 2026/27 singles rulebook sets 2 × 16 kg for Women Open, 2 × 24 kg for Men Open and Women Pro, and 2 × 32 kg for Men Pro.[1]

Kettlebell training studies report gains in strength and power. Six weeks of twice-weekly kettlebell swings improved half-squat one-repetition maximum and vertical jump in 21 men, with no significant difference from jump-squat training.[2] In a 6-week comparison, weightlifting produced greater strength gains than kettlebell training, and neither changed body composition.[3]

A 2025 study of a simulated HYROX race reported that faster finish times correlated with higher maximal oxygen uptake, more endurance training volume and lower body fat.[6]

What the rulebook and studies report

  1. 1Kettlebell farmers carry: the standard
  2. 2Kettlebell swing
  3. 3Kettlebell snatch intervals
  4. 4Kettlebell versus weightlifting

  1. 1Kettlebell farmers carry: the standard

    In the sources
    Both kettlebells are carried at all times while moving, with arms extended by the sides; putting them down to rest is permitted if they do not move forward.[1]

  2. 2Kettlebell swing

    In the sources
    6 weeks, twice a week, 12 rounds of 30 seconds of swings with 30 seconds' rest: half-squat 1RM and jump height increased.[2]

  3. 3Kettlebell snatch intervals

    In the sources
    In 17 female collegiate soccer players, 4 weeks of 20-minute sessions of 15-second snatch and rest intervals raised maximal oxygen uptake by about 6% in the kettlebell group; a circuit weight-training group did not change.[4]

  4. 4Kettlebell versus weightlifting

    In the sources
    Weightlifting produced greater back-squat strength gains over 6 weeks; vertical jump did not differ between groups.[3]

Rules, penalties and study limits

The rulebook states that each missing farmers carry lap brings a 3-minute penalty, kettlebells not returned upright to the correct area bring a 30-second penalty, and completing the station with the incorrect weight requires repeating it, with disqualification if not repeated. Chalk is permitted only at the sled pull and farmers carry.[1]

A 2019 scoping review of 99 kettlebell publications rated confidence in reported effects as low to very low, with effect sizes typically trivial to small and longitudinal studies largely underpowered.[5]

Frequently asked questions

Which HYROX station uses kettlebells?

The farmers carry, over 200 m, according to the official 2026/27 singles rulebook.[1]

What kettlebell weights are used at HYROX?

The 2026/27 singles rulebook sets 2 × 16 kg for Women Open, 2 × 24 kg for Men Open and Women Pro, and 2 × 32 kg for Men Pro.[1]

What have kettlebell training studies found?

Swing training improved maximal and explosive strength in 21 men;[2] weightlifting produced greater strength gains than kettlebell training;[3] and snatch intervals raised maximal oxygen uptake in female soccer players.[4]

How strong is the kettlebell research?

A 2019 scoping review rated confidence in reported effects as low to very low and described most longitudinal studies as underpowered.[5]

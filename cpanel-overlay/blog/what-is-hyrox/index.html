What is HYROX? The format, rules and published data

HYROX's format, stations, weights, divisions and penalties from the official rulebook, with HYROX's own race-science data and third-party finish times.

By Susmit Jalan, coach at Online Fitness Worldwide. Updated 20 November 2026

HYROX describes itself as a fitness race of 1 km of running followed by one workout station, repeated 8 times. Its singles rulebook for the 2026/27 season states that the running distance is the same across divisions, 1 km between each station for a total of 8 km, and that racers must be at least 16 years old on race day.[1][2]

HYROX's India site states that over 80 global races in 2025 saw over 550,000 athletes and 350,000 spectators. This is the organiser's own figure.[3]

In a simulated Open race reported in HYROX's 2025 Sports Science Report, 11 recreational athletes had a median completion time of 86.2 minutes, of which running accounted for 51.2 minutes and the stations 32.8 minutes; 70.5% of the race was at a heart rate classed as hard or very hard (70–100% of maximum).[5]

The format, as the official rules state it

  3. 3Divisions
  4. 4Penalties
  5. 5Age groups

  1. 1The 8 stations, in order

    In the sources
    SkiErg 1,000 m; sled push 50 m (4 × 12.5 m); sled pull 50 m (4 × 12.5 m); burpee broad jumps 80 m; rowing 1,000 m; farmers carry 200 m; sandbag lunges 100 m; wall balls, 100 reps.[2]

  2. 2Weights by division (2026/27)

    In the sources
    Women Open / Men Open and Women Pro / Men Pro: sled push 102 / 152 / 202 kg including sled; sled pull 78 / 103 / 153 kg; farmers carry 2 × 16 / 2 × 24 / 2 × 32 kg; lunge sandbag 10 / 20 / 30 kg; wall ball 4 / 6 / 9 kg. Wall-ball target height: 2.70 m for women, 3.00 m for men.[2]

  3. 3Divisions

    In the sources
    The singles rulebook lists Women, Women Pro, Men and Men Pro. The HYROX site also lists Doubles (two racers run together and split each station as they choose) and Relay (a team of four, each running 2 × 1 km and doing one station).[1][2]

  4. 4Penalties

    In the sources
    The rulebook states that missing an entire station or 1 km run leads to disqualification, and that entering or leaving the Roxzone through the wrong arch brings a 2-minute penalty. Station protocols list, for example, 15-second penalties after a warning, 3 minutes per missing sled lane or farmers-carry lap, and a 980 m minimum on the SkiErg and rower.[2]

  5. 5Age groups

    In the sources
    Official age groups run from Under 24 (16–24) in five-year bands to 85–89, by age on race day. Athletes aged 60 and above qualify for the World Championships via the Open division only.[2]

Figures from official and third-party sources

RoxTimes, a third party, reports from 1,370,103 official finishes (seasons 2024/25 to 2026/27) a typical Open Men finish of 1:27:55 and Open Women of 1:36:01, with the fastest 10% under 1:10:36 and 1:18:24.[6]

HYROX's race finder, read for this update, carried 70 event listings, including INDUSIND BANK HYROX Gujarat on 26–28 November 2026 and HYROX Noida on 15–17 January 2027.[4] The Gujarat event page gives the venue as Helipad Exhibition Center, Gandhinagar.[7]

What the rules and guidelines state on safety

ACSM's 2015 screening model bases pre-exercise medical clearance on current activity, signs or symptoms and/or known cardiovascular, metabolic or renal disease, and desired intensity.[8]

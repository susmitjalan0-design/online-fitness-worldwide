# Translation glossary: race posts

Source copy is written to translate cleanly: short sentences, no idioms, units and numbers written the same way in every language (kg first, pounds in brackets). Arabic is right to left. Latin names and numbers inside Arabic text are wrapped so they keep their order.

Keep these in Latin script in every language: HYROX, DEKA, ATHX, Spartan, CrossFit, SkiErg, RowErg, RAM, MetCon X, FIT, MILE, STRONG, ATLAS, LITE, PRO, Open, Roxzone, Iron Hour, Lean Reset, Return, Open Floor.

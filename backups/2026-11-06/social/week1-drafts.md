## LI-1. Wed Nov 11. The desk-day week (repurpose S2; blog B)
Most desk days look the same.
Long sits. Short walks. A stiff neck by four.

WHO and the NHS give the same advice: sit less, break up long sits, and train every major muscle group on at least two days a week.

You don't need a gym for that. A chair, a desk, a backpack and a band will do.

Here is how I would coach the week:

Every workday
- Walk breaks every 20 to 30 minutes
- One set of band lateral raises

Two days a week (Monday and Thursday work well)
- Sit-to-stand
- Backpack hip hinge
- Band or backpack row
- Desk push-up
Three rounds.

After the last call
- Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch, 30 to 60 seconds a side

Typing keeps your arms in front of you for hours. The row trains the other direction.

I coach this live, 1:1 or in a small group, on video, with clients in different time zones. The seven moves, the doses and the safety notes are in the post:
https://onlinefitnessworldwide.com/blog/best-exercises-for-desk-workers

General information, not medical advice. Sharp, spreading or worsening pain: check with a doctor or physio first.

## LI-2. Sun Nov 15. The break is not a reset (repurpose S5; blog E)
Back at it on Monday after a long gap?
Start where you are, not where you were.

Three facts worth knowing, each with its limits.

1. The break is not a full reset. In one small trial of 14 young men, three-week breaks between training blocks did not stop them matching the muscle and strength gains of men who trained straight through for 24 weeks. Small study. Short breaks. But the pattern comes back faster than you think.

2. Strength counts on its own. A meta-analysis of cohort studies linked muscle-strengthening activity with a 10 to 17% lower risk of death from any cause. It is observational. A good reason, not a guarantee.

3. You don't start at the target. You start with the walks and two strength days.

My first-month plan:
Weeks 1 and 2: walk 10 to 15 minutes most days. Two strength days. One or two rounds. Stop each set with 2 or 3 reps in the tank.
Weeks 3 and 4: walk 15 to 20 minutes. Two or three strength days. Two or three rounds.

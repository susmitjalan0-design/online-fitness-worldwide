## One-session production notes
**Bring:** phone, stack of books, mat, chalk or bar, band, backpack, chair, desk edge, strap, laptop, notepad, soft side window or one lamp.

**Shot list (faceless):**
1. Phone on books, hip height, facing mat
2. Side squat / hinge, knees to floor (face out)
3. Same move with camera too high / too close / backlit / feet cropped
4. Overhead or tight: hands on bar, chalk, bar over mid-foot
5. Feet marching; leg swing; light ramp sets (hands + plates)
6. Calf or hip stretch, hands/feet only
7. Desk kit: walk-break feet, band raises, sit-to-stand, backpack hinge, row, desk push-up, hip flexor
8. Empty chair + laptop calendar + notepad (slow push-in)
9. Empty rack / clean bone wall for post 10 design

**Design pass:** bone/cream frames, ink Outfit-style type, thin `#25D366` rules; square for singles/carousels, 9:16 for Reels.

**Do not film for this pack:** talking-head, client faces, trophies, prices, credential logos.

# Community Playbook v1 — Online Fitness Worldwide

**Draft for Susmit to review.** Nothing in this playbook is posted, messaged, or moderated until you approve and send it yourself. Brand: Online Fitness Worldwide / OnlineFitnessWorldwide only. Audience: worldwide live online clients (US, UK, Europe, Middle East, Asia, Australia — India is one market among many, never the default).

---

## Open questions for Susmit

These are listed, not assumed. Answers will shape v2.

1. **Where does the member group live?** WhatsApp, Discord, Circle, Slack, Facebook Group, or something else?
2. **Who hosts live member events?** You, a rotating member host helper, or a mix?
3. **When does the welcome-into-the-group invite go out?** After Day 7, after Day 14, after the first Open Floor, or only when the client asks?
4. **Are Open Floor clients and 1:1 clients in the same group, or separate spaces?**
5. **Should race-week send-offs be optional opt-in posts in the group, or a separate short live call?**
6. **Who can invite someone into a member role (welcomer, training buddy, Open Floor host helper)?** You only, or can current role-holders nominate?
7. **Do you want a quiet "lurk allowed" norm, or a gentle expectation to check in once a week?**
8. **Any topics that are always off-limits in the group beyond medical advice and body-shaming?** (e.g. politics, religion, selling side products)
9. **How should we handle a member who wants to leave the group but stay as a coaching client?** Soft exit script?
10. **Preferred language for celebrating milestones** — short group shout-out only, or also a private note from you?

---

## 1. Community charter

### Purpose (one sentence)

Help Online Fitness Worldwide clients feel they belong with people who train with the same coach — so showing up, asking questions, and celebrating real progress gets easier.

*(Primary job = client success and belonging; public acquisition is a by-product, not the pitch — Spinks, The Business of Belonging.)*

### Values (4)

1. **Show up for each other** — Consistency beats intensity. A quiet "I trained today" is enough.
2. **Respect every body and every stage** — Beginners and masters athletes share the same space. No ranking people by how they look or how fast they finish.
3. **Honest, not performative** — Missed sessions and hard weeks are welcome. Belonging cues and shared vulnerability make groups safer (Coyle, The Culture Code).
4. **Coach-led boundaries, member-led warmth** — Clear norms protect the room; members keep it human (Parker, The Art of Gathering — generous authority).

### Member norms

- **Respect.** Disagree on training preferences; never attack the person.
- **No body-shaming.** Not about yourself as a joke, and not about anyone else.
- **No medical advice between members.** Pain, injury, swelling, dizziness, or "should I still train?" → hand straight to Physio or a qualified clinician. Peers may share *their own* story only if asked; never prescribe.
- **Consent before sharing anyone's details.** Photos, race times, programme names, location, or progress → written opt-in first (see §8).
- **No selling.** No hustles, affiliate links, or recruiting for other programmes inside the group.
- **Keep it effortless.** Short posts beat essays. Reply when you can; lurking is fine unless Susmit sets a different expectation (open question #7).

---

## 2. Welcome ritual

**When:** After Retention's Day 0–14 onboarding is underway (tech setup done, first sessions landed, week-2 nudge sent). Community does **not** replace Retention's 1:1 welcome — it adds an optional group door.

**Rule:** Opt-in only. Never add someone to the group without a clear yes.

### Flow (draft for Susmit)

1. Retention (or you) sends a private invite after ~Day 10–14.
2. Client replies yes / not yet / no thanks.
3. On yes: add them to the group platform (once chosen — open question #1).
4. Post a short welcome (below) **only if they also opt in to being named**.
5. A welcomer (if one is active) DMs a one-line hello within 48 hours.

### Draft — private invite (Susmit sends)

> Hi [Client name] — you're a couple of weeks in, which is a good moment to offer the member group.  
> It's a small worldwide chat for Online Fitness Worldwide clients: show-up notes, questions, and the odd live hangout across time zones. Optional. No pressure to post.  
> Want in? Reply **yes**, **not yet**, or **no thanks**.  
> If yes, tell me whether you're happy to be welcomed by first name in the group, or you'd rather join quietly. — Susmit

### Draft — group welcome (only with name consent)

> Welcome [Client name] to the Online Fitness Worldwide member group.  
> [Client name] trains with us online and is based in [region or time zone — only if client agreed to share].  
> If you're around, say hi — no essay required.

### Draft — quiet join (name not shared)

> A new member just joined. Please make them feel at home if they post. (They're keeping a low profile for now — that is completely fine.)

*(Coleman’s early touchpoints and Vogl’s initiation idea: a clear doorway into the group, not a silent add.)*

## 3. Member roles

Start small. Hand people real roles instead of doing everything as the coach (*Get Together* — Richardson, Huynh, Sotto). Roles are invitations, not jobs with prices or perks lists.

| Role | What they do | How to invite |
|---|---|---|
| **Welcomer** | Within 48h of a consented welcome post, sends a short private hello and answers "where do I find X?" questions. Does not give training or medical advice. | After ~1 month of friendly presence: private note from Susmit asking if they'd like to try it for a month. |
| **Training buddy** | Optional pair (or trio) in a similar time-zone band who check in on planned sessions once or twice a week. Peer support only — plans stay with the coach. | Offer after someone asks for accountability, or match two opt-ins. Always mutual yes. |
| **Open Floor host helper** | For live Open Floor / member events: greets arrivals in chat, posts the session purpose at the top, helps with mute/camera etiquette, flags time so the coach can close cleanly. | Invite regular Open Floor clients who already model the culture. Rotate; nobody owns it forever. |

### Draft — role invite

> Hi [Client name] — you've been a steady presence in the group, and I wondered if you'd try the **[role]** role for the next month.  
> It means: [one-sentence duty from the table].  
> No extra sessions, no public title required. Totally fine to say no or "not now". — Susmit

### Inner rings (light touch)

Vogl describes inner rings as deeper layers of belonging, not status ladders. Here that means: members who host, welcome, or buddy are trusted helpers — not a VIP tier, not a results club, and never a public ranking.

1. **One live member event** (45–60 min) — purpose-led, not "a catch-up" (Parker).
2. **One non-competitive challenge** (1–2 weeks) — see §5.
3. **One recap** — short written summary of what happened; celebrate only consented highlights.

## 5. Four sample challenge formats

All challenges are **non-competitive**. No prizes with prices. No personal-data leaderboards, ranked tables, or public streak dashboards. Share progress only if the member chooses.

### Challenge 1 — Consistency streak (beginner-friendly)

- **Aim:** Train on your planned days for 7–14 days.
- **How:** Daily or end-of-week check-in: "Hit my sessions" / "Partial" / "Reset tomorrow".
- **Scale:** Beginners count any planned session; masters athletes may add a mobility closer — still no ranking.
- **Coach line:** Missed a day? Restart the count without shame.

### Challenge 2 — Movement-skill practice (spacing + retrieval)

Drawn from *Make It Stick* (Brown, Roediger, McDaniel): practice that is spaced and asks you to retrieve cues sticks better than one long cram.

- **Aim:** One skill for two weeks (e.g. hinge, goblet squat, row sequence, wall-ball catch).
- **How:** Short practice 3–4 days/week. Mid-week and end-week: without looking at notes, write or voice the 2–3 cues you use.
- **Scale:** Beginners use bodyweight; advanced add load only if already in their plan.
- **Coach line:** Hybrid Race Coach / Masters owns technique progressions; this challenge only reinforces cues already taught.

### Challenge 3 — Team distance (any cardio)

- **Aim:** Collective kilometres (or minutes) on any cardio members already do — run, row, bike, walk, ski-erg.
- **How:** Members optionally log distance/minutes toward a shared group total you announce without naming individuals unless they opt in.
- **Scale:** Walking counts. Race athletes may log easy aerobic only — no "push for the board".
- **Safety:** Pain during cardio → stop and route to Physio / clinician. No prescribing intensity in the group chat.

### Challenge 4 — Race-prep station practice (HYROX / DEKA / ATHX)

- **Aim:** Rehearse one station pattern already in the member's plan (e.g. sled pacing feel, farmer carry sets, erg splits as technique focus).
- **How:** 10–15 min skill block, 2–3 times in the fortnight. Share one learning, not a time to beat.
- **Scale:** Beginners use lighter or substitute patterns from their coach; masters athletes adjust volume per Masters guidance.
- **Boundary:** Programming stays with Hybrid Race Coach / Masters. Community hosts the shared practice window and peer encouragement only.

### Draft — challenge kickoff

> **[Month] challenge: [name]**  
> Why: [one line].  
> How long: [dates].  
> How to join: reply **I'm in**.  
> How to share: optional — no rankings, no required screenshots.  
> Pain or injury questions: message Susmit; we'll loop in Physio / a clinician — please don't crowd-diagnose here.

## 6. Virtual race-day meetup / send-off

For members entered in **HYROX / DEKA / ATHX** (or similar). Purpose: connection and calm — not last-minute programming (Heath brothers: moments of elevation and connection; Parker: strong opening and closing).

### Format options (pick one per race week — open question #5)

**A. Live send-off (20–30 min, UTC announced)**  
1. Open with purpose: "We're here to send [Client name(s)] into race week."  
2. Each athlete (opt-in) shares one word for how they feel + one thing they're trusting in their plan.  
3. Peers offer short encouragement — no technique overhauls, no new sessions.  
4. Close: "Trust the work. Message the group when you're done if you want."

**B. Async send-off post**  
> Race week: sending good energy to [Client name] for [event] on [date, timezone].  
> Reply with a short note if you like — keep it kind and light.  
> [Client name]: no need to reply; focus on your taper and your checklist.

### Hard rules for race week

- No new workouts invented in the chat.
- Taper and station plans stay with Hybrid Race Coach / Masters.
- Nerves and confidence language can nod to Mindset; Community does not run a mental-skills workshop in the thread.
- Results posts after the race: **only with written consent** (§8). Never invent finish times or placings.

## 7. Moderation and safety

Susmit (or a named host) holds generous authority: set norms, protect the group, don't stay passive when harm shows up (Parker).

### Conflict

1. Assume good intent once.
2. Private message both parties; ask for facts, not a public trial.
3. Restate the relevant norm.
4. If it continues: temporary mute / cool-off, then decide on removal.
5. Never debate someone's body, identity, or worth in-thread.

### Spam / selling / off-topic blasts

1. Delete or hide the post (per platform tools).
2. Private warning with the "no selling" norm.
3. Repeat → remove from group; coaching relationship handled separately by Retention if needed.

### Pain / injury / medical questions

**Always hand to Physio or a qualified clinician.** Community and peer members do not diagnose, suggest rehab progressions, or say "push through".

**Draft reply (public or private):**

> Thanks for flagging that, [Client name]. Pain and injury questions sit with a qualified clinician / our Physio lane — I don't want the group guessing. I'll follow up with you 1:1 and we'll get you the right next step. Please pause the movement that provoked it until then.

### Crisis / self-harm / emergency

If someone signals acute distress or emergency: urge local emergency services / appropriate local helpline; notify Susmit immediately. Do not run therapy in the group.

### Tone when moderating

Short, warm, clear (*Writing for Busy Readers*). Say what happens next. No public shaming.

---

## 8. Consent rules for milestones and spotlights

Memorable moments of pride and connection are worth designing (Heath & Heath, *The Power of Moments*) — and only with permission.

### Written opt-in (required) before any of:

- Using their name in a group welcome or shout-out  
- Sharing a photo, race result, before/after, or progress note  
- Featuring them in a Social post (hand to Social only after consent)  
- Quoting them as a testimonial  

### Draft — consent ask

> Hi [Client name] — would you be open to a short shout-out in the member group about [milestone in their words]?  
> I would post roughly: "[draft sentence]".  
> Reply **yes**, **yes but keep my surname/location private**, or **no**.  
> You can change your mind later and I'll take it down. — Susmit

### Easy opt-out

- "Please take that down" → remove within 24 hours, no debate.
- Leaving the group does not require an explanation.
- Never invent members, stories, results, or testimonials. Use `[Client name]` placeholders in drafts until real consent exists.

### What we never publish without consent

Location finer than a broad region they offered, medical history, body-weight numbers, screenshots of DMs, or another member's comments about them.

## 9. Hand-offs

Community owns the **many-to-many** layer. It does not swallow other bots' jobs.

| Hand to | When |
|---|---|
| **Retention** | 1:1 adherence, weekly check-ins, Day 0–30 onboarding, at-risk save playbooks, quiet clients who need a private nudge |
| **Social** | Anything meant for the public internet (IG/site/blog). Community drafts stay inside the member space until Susmit approves a public version |
| **Nurture** | Pre-clients and enquire follow-ups. Do not add prospects to the member group |
| **Mindset** | Mental-skills content, confidence frameworks, race-week nerves beyond a simple send-off |
| **Hybrid Race Coach / Masters** | Training plans, taper, station progressions, masters adaptations |
| **Physio** | Pain, injury, return-to-training clearance language, any medical-adjacent question |
| **Nutrition** | Nutrition coaching questions (Community does not give nutrition-therapy advice) |
| **Video** | Technique demo requests that need a filmed clip |

### Draft — hand-off line (in-thread)

> Good question — that's a [Retention / Physio / Hybrid Race Coach / …] topic, so I'll take it there and follow up with you directly. Keeping this thread for peer support and show-ups.
